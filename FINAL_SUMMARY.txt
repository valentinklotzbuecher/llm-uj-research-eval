================================================================================
CRITICAL MISSING FEATURE: Krippendorff's Alpha
================================================================================

The most important missing statistical analysis is Krippendorff's alpha (α).

WHY IT MATTERS:
  - Inter-rater reliability metric specifically designed for interval scales
  - Handles any number of raters (better than Cohen's κ for many-raters)
  - Naturally handles missing data
  - Most appropriate for research evaluation tasks
  - Allows comparison of LLM-Human agreement vs Human-Human agreement

CURRENT STATE:
  - results.qmd has Cohen's κ (quadratic and unweighted)
  - slides_vk has BOTH Cohen's κ AND Krippendorff's α
  - This provides much better interpretability

IMPLEMENTATION:
  - Requires: library("irr")
  - Use: irr::kripp.alpha(M, method="interval")
  - Code provided in code_snippets_ready_to_use.md
