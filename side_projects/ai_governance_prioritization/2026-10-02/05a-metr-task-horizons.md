# Measuring AI Ability to Complete Long Software Tasks — METR / Kwa et al.

**Checked 8 October 2026. High-priority recency exception.** [Paper, arXiv 2503.14499](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.14499); NeurIPS 2025. Its July 10, 2026 v4 change was only an author-list correction, not a fresh research revision.

**Verified policy-synthesis uptake:** The [International AI Safety Report 2026](https://internationalaisafetyreport.org/publication/international-ai-safety-report-2026) directly uses the task-horizon result and approximately seven-month doubling trend. This is evidence being incorporated into a policymaker-facing synthesis, not proof of national-government endorsement.

**Unjournal value:** Independent audit of task-suite composition, human-time baselines, logistic estimation, variation by scaffold and task type, uncertainty around trend and extrapolation. Separate the original 2025 design from later [METR tracker updates](https://metr.org/time-horizons/).
