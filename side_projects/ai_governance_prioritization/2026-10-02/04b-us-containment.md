# U.S. Technological Containment and the Rise of China's Open AI Ecosystem

Evidence refreshed October 5, 2026. AI-assisted prioritization note; not an official evaluation.

**Wang Jin, Nadav Kunievsky, Bowen Lou, Tianshu Sun, and James Evans. Proposed queue: about 15.**

The paper studies whether U.S. technology restrictions were followed by a greater Chinese emphasis on open AI infrastructure and wider diffusion of Chinese-origin models. Primary record: https://ideas.repec.org/p/arx/papers/2606.15999.html

**Attention:** the broad policy question is prominent, but I did not verify a government document, major independent news story, or funder decision that specifically relies on this paper. Research indexing and the August revision are verified. Do not treat attention to the general U.S.-China AI debate as paper-specific uptake.

**What an evaluation could add:** an innovation or trade economist plus a China technology-policy specialist could check timing, comparison groups, repository measures, parallel policy changes, diffusion measures, and alternative explanations. The current title is more cautious than the paper's earlier framing, so the latest version should be frozen before review.

**Bottom line:** potentially important and empirically contestable, but below candidates with clearer downstream use.
