# Acting with AI: An Interaction-Based Framework for Agentic Tort Liability — Yiheng Yao

**Checked 8 October 2026. Medium-low priority; better in a legal synthesis than a flagship standalone review.** [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.00518).

The paper distinguishes pure tool use, collaborative planning, and autonomous drift, then relates those interactions to different tort doctrines. It proposes interaction logs as a central evidence source and a Reasonable Agent standard.
