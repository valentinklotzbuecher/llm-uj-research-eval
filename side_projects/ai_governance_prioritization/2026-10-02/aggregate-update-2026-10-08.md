## Auditing standard

Always label **documented policy analysis**, **official decision use**, **funding/commissioning**, **institutional dissemination**, **independent press/third-party commentary**, and **author promotion or scholarly citation** separately. Absence of a verified use in a bounded search is not proof that no such use exists.
