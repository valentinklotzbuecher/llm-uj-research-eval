# Existing shortlist

These seven completed prioritization notes are indexed here. This is not an official Unjournal evaluation.

- [Forecasting the Economic Effects of AI](https://www.nber.org/papers/w35046) — first commission; [Yale Budget Lab](https://budgetlab.yale.edu/research/what-might-ai-adoption-mean-fiscal-and-economic-outlook) uses the scenarios, and [Third Way](https://www.thirdway.org/memo/how-will-ai-impact-the-federal-budget) cites the resulting estimates. These are downstream *policy analyses*, not government adoption. NBER discloses philanthropic funding and CBO seminar feedback separately.
- [Diversion and resale](https://epoch.ai/publications/chip-smuggling) — second; high-value measurement audit. [Specialist coverage](https://www.tomshardware.com/tech-industry/artificial-intelligence/billions-worth-of-export-restricted-ai-accelerators-sold-to-china-report-details-how-chinese-firms-skirt-trumps-regulations) reuses the estimate; a copy on Samaritan's site is not independent validation.
- [The AGI Race and Existential Risk](https://www.nber.org/papers/w35276) — third; formal economics review. [Chicago BFI brief](https://bfi.uchicago.edu/insights/the-agi-race-and-existential-risk/) on September 30 is institutional dissemination, not official uptake.
- [Cooperating against Catastrophe](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28291) — paired theory review; recent preprint, policy use not verified.
- [Governing Artificial Intelligence: Public Preferences](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14585) — survey-methods reserve. No government consultation use verified.
- [Overcoming Judgment-Proofness](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=6173619) — insurance-law reserve. No paper-specific insurer/regulator adoption verified.
- [When Does Regulation by Insurance Work?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.06597) — paired with Weil. [Author-written Lawfare piece](https://www.lawfaremedia.org/article/healthy-insurance-markets-will-be-critical-for-ai-governance) is promotion, not independent validation.

See the dated aggregate update for attention categories and supporting evidence.
**Key evaluation questions:** FRI—elicitation and downstream uncertainty; compute estimate—overlap and sensitivity; AGI race/cooperation—equilibrium assumptions; public preferences—sampling, attribute framing and representativeness; insurance pair—risk pooling, incentives and correlated loss. Detailed notes are also available in the earlier October 7 conversation bundle. Author responsiveness and funder decisions remain unverified.
