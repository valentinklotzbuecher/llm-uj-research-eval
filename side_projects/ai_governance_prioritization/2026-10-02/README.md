## Recommended first commissions

**Start with FRI's Forecasting the Economic Effects of AI, Epoch's compute-smuggling estimate, and The AGI Race and Existential Risk.** These offer three different opportunities: improve research already used in applied policy analysis; audit an influential but difficult-to-observe quantity; and test a consequential theoretical mechanism with economists well matched to the work.

Next consider **The Economics of Recursive Self-Improvement**, **Delays to Frontier AI in the EU and UK**, and **METR's task-horizon research**, the last with an explicit recency and scope exception. Review the intelligence-explosion policy report alongside its economic and empirical inputs, rather than allowing visibility alone to determine the allocation of review effort.

## What the attention checks changed

**FRI has the clearest verified research-to-analysis pathway.** [Yale's Budget Lab](https://budgetlab.yale.edu/research/what-might-ai-adoption-mean-fiscal-and-economic-outlook) used the forecasts in fiscal scenarios and later corrected its translation between two productivity measures. This is not automatically an error in the FRI study. [NBER acknowledgments](https://www.nber.org/papers/w35046) separately identify philanthropic funding and CBO seminar feedback. Funding, engagement, and downstream analysis are different facts; none establishes that government policy changed.

**Epoch's estimate has identifiable public reuse.** [September specialist reporting](https://www.tomshardware.com/tech-industry/artificial-intelligence/billions-worth-of-export-restricted-ai-accelerators-sold-to-china-report-details-how-chinese-firms-skirt-trumps-regulations) repeats the estimate while primarily covering another investigation. The [guest article on Peter Wildeford's site](https://blog.peterwildeford.com/p/how-banned-ai-chips-end-up-in-china) is by Erich Grunewald. Neither establishes independent validation of the estimate by a government.

**METR deserves an explicit exception rather than a disguised recency claim.** The [International AI Safety Report](https://internationalaisafetyreport.org/publication/2026-report-extended-summary-policymakers) uses the task-horizon research. Its [tracker](https://metr.org/time-horizons/) also acknowledges limitations and corrections. A new evaluation should add independent analysis, not merely repeat the FAQ.

**Some less publicized work has meaningful connections to research users.** [AI in a Fragmenting World](https://www.nber.org/papers/w35597) acknowledges UK and South Korean government research funding. [September 24 Brookings commentary](https://www.brookings.edu/articles/in-ai-evaluation-access-is-not-evidence/) directly links Toward an Evaluation Science. Support and citation are important, but neither is proof of institutional adoption.

## Changes to the original shortlist

Move FRI and Epoch ahead of the competition model under the requested emphasis on actual attention and use. Retain Fudenberg and Koh for a paired comparison. Keep Lundgren and Tallberg as a strong empirical reserve. Put Weil and Trout into a conditional joint liability-and-insurance scoping review unless a named policy or insurance-design user identifies an immediate question.

Add the recursive-self-improvement and European-release-delay studies to the first commissioning discussion. Keep a lower-cost route for measurement audits, causal-interpretation checks, and evaluation-infrastructure synthesis. A highly visible policy paper need not receive the same kind of review as an empirical study or a formal model.

## Reading and inference limits

These are prioritization reports, not full research evaluations. Reading depth varies and is disclosed per paper. Some primary reports and downstream analyses were read substantially; many candidates were screened from primary abstracts and institutional summaries. No dataset replication or complete proof audit was performed in this round.

A paper citation does not validate the paper. The proposed checks are questions, not established defects. A seminar is not adoption; funding is not endorsement; an author's job is not paper-specific uptake; the absence of verified attention in a bounded search is not proof that no attention exists. Old Altmetric or citation totals have not been relabeled as current.

Public preprints, revisions, and code can make an evaluation easier, but they do not establish likely author responsiveness. Before commissioning, request the current version, relevant materials, known substantive reviews, and a concrete description of the decisions the work is informing. Keep sensitive outreach and conflict notes separate from this public collection.
