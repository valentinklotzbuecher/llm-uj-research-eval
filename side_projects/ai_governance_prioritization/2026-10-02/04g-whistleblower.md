# Insights for an AI Whistleblower Office from 30 Case Studies

Evidence refreshed October 5, 2026. AI-assisted prioritization note; not an official evaluation.

**Ethan Beri and Mauricio Baker. Proposed queue: about 20.**

Primary source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.01245

The paper uses 30 historical whistleblower cases to derive recommendations for an AI-specific program. Specialist accountability coverage exists, but broader independent press was not verified. U.S. legislative interest in AI whistleblower protection is real, yet I did not verify that lawmakers relied on this paper. Policy proximity is therefore not counted as uptake. Paper-specific funding was not verified.

A public-administration or labor-law scholar should audit case selection, survivorship and publication bias, feature coding, denominators behind percentages, cross-industry comparability, and how strongly the evidence supports specific design recommendations.

Recommendation: useful lower-cost review, but not first-wave.
