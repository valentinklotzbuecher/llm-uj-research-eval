# National AI regulation framework

Evidence refreshed October 5, 2026. This is an AI-assisted prioritization note, not an official evaluation.

The paper proposes a source-traceable protocol for comparing national AI policy portfolios. Current external uptake appears limited. A useful review would focus on criterion construction, scoring anchors, source completeness, comparability, and sensitivity to weighting.

Recommendation: low priority for a full evaluation unless a government, international organization, comparative-index project, or funder adopts the framework.

Primary source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.15417
