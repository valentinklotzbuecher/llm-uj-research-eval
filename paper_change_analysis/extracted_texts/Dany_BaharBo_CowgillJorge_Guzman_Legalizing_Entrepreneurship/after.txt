ABSTRACT
We use administrative data linked to the complete formal business registry to study a 2018 policy
shift in Colombia that made nearly half a million Venezuelan undocumented migrants eligible for
a resident visa. Immigrants who receive the visa increase their economic activity in the form of
higher entrepreneurship by a factor as high as 12, bringing it to parity with native Colombians
four years later. To establish causal estimates, we develop a novel extension of a regression
discontinuity design. Our design uses variation in the running-variable (coming from rain) to
instrument for migrants’ choices to apply for visas.
